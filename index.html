PROTO-VALT HUNTER

You are Proto-Valt, hunter of lost vaults. Grab keys 🗝️, dodge beasts, crack the vault 🏦 and escape through the portal!

📱 Phone & Tablet ready 👆 Touch + ⌨️ Keyboard 🏆 Best: 0
How to play
• Move: ◀ ▶ or AD / ←→
• Jump / double-jump: ⬆ or Space / W
• Attack: ⚔️ or J / tap enemies
• Collect all keys → open the vault (walk up to it) → enter the portal 🌀
• Avoid spikes & beasts. 3 hearts ❤ — hearts 🧡 heal you.

Tip: landscape = bigger view. Sound on for vault fanfare 🔊

FIELD GUIDE

HOW TO PLAY

🪙 Coins = +10 pts. Big coins = +50.
🗝️ Keys — you need every key on the level to crack the vault.
🏦 Vault — stand in front & press Up / Jump (or tap vault) to loot +500.
🌀 Portal opens after the vault. Walk in to finish the level.
👾 Crawlers patrol platforms. Bats swoop. Spikes hurt — jump over!
⚔️ Attack has short range but kills in 1 hit (+50 pts). You can also stomp bats.
❤ You have 3 hearts. Red flash = invincible for a moment. Find 🧡 to heal.
📱 Phone/tablet: hold ◀ ▶ to run, tap ⬆ to jump (again mid-air = double jump), tap ⚔️ to swing.
